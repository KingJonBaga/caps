ABOUT NURTURA

Maternal care, connected through every step.

NURTURA is a maternal health management prototype designed to help patients and care teams keep important parts of care organized in one place.

Appointments

Keep upcoming visits and care follow-ups visible to the patient and care team.

Health records

Review maternal health information and records in the patient workspace.

Connected care

Patient, care-provider, and administrator views support different parts of the workflow.

OUR COMMUNITY

Different roles. One circle of care.

Maternal care works best when people can stay informed and connected. Each role has a dedicated area in the prototype.

For patients

View a personal dashboard, appointments, pregnancy records, and postpartum follow-up information.

For care providers

Review patient information, appointments, and follow-up items in the care-team workspace.

For administrators

Use the administration workspace to review sample accounts and prototype reports.

This is an academic prototype with sample data. It is not a medical record system and does not provide medical advice.
